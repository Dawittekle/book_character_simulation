Lorely Logo Redesign Proposals

Three literary evolutions of the signature 8-petal geometric mark. Tailored specifically for classical book simulation, cognitive characters, and voice dialogue while preserving existing brand equity and silhouette recognition.

1. The Literary Rosette

Highest Continuity

Maintains the exact 8 outer circular nodes from the current logo, but replaces the generic central dot with an open book folio. Represents characters and cognitive memory orbiting out of classical literature.

  • Preserves 100% of current silhouette recognition
  • Instantly signals literary & book focus
  • Scales down cleanly to 16px favicon

2. The Folio Petals

Tactile & Organic

Sculpts each of the 8 floral petals into an open book leaf / deckled manuscript folio, terminating in the original circular node. Radiates like an unfolded volume in full bloom.

  • Deeply artistic and editorial
  • Evokes the feeling of turning pages
  • Rich tactile storytelling geometry

3. The Dialogue Octofoil

Conversational Dialogue

Evolves the 8 nodes into dialogue quotation glyphs / speech seeds rotating around an editorial chapter asterisk. Focuses on the act of conversation and spoken dialogue with characters.

  • Direct representation of voice & dialogue
  • Editorial chapter ornament in center
  • Expresses back-and-forth literary exchanges
